03

Questioning Authority & Communication Method

How MD, CEO, Admin & Departments resolve questions while protecting agent morale & retention

Simulating leadership query workflow...
Step 1/3
Step 1 • Data First 01 / 03

Check Shift Data First

Departments must first verify the WhatsApp Group Logs and EOD Tracker Sheet to understand the agent's flexi-shift schedule before asking any questions.

✓ Eliminates unnecessary timing questions
Step 2 • Manager Bridge 02 / 03

Ask Operational Manager First

Any operational question must be asked to the Operational Manager first—who then communicates with agents using established rapport.

★ Avoids sudden agent frustration & prevents employee turnover.
Step 3 • Channel Rule 03 / 03

DM First, Call Second

All internal communication must happen first via Direct Message (DM). A phone call should be made only if there is no response to the DM.

✓ Zero disruption during live client sales calls

04 • Operational Manager Absence Contingency

When the Operational Manager is Absent / On Leave

Other departments may step in to manage operational requirements directly—ensuring zero disturbance to the sales workflow while maintaining calm, respectful DM-first communication without causing sudden frustration to agents.
